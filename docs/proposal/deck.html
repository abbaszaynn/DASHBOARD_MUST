01 — The Problem

Three gaps in current capability

Latency

Content that incites sectarian hostility circulates widely before it is noticed. By the time a complaint arrives, the administration's options have narrowed.

Language

Much local discourse is Roman Urdu or regional — Shina, Balti, Burushaski. General-purpose commercial moderation tools handle these poorly or not at all.

Evidence

When a case is escalated, its legal basis is assembled manually and inconsistently. Officers need the applicable provision identified alongside the content.

A fourth consideration governs the design. In a region with real sectarian sensitivity, a system that produces false accusations is worse than no system at all.

02 — How It Works

Six agents. One terminus.

Each component performs one task and passes an enriched case record onward. Processing ends, in every case, at human review.

01IngestionLicensed provider. Public content only.
→
02ClassificationXLM-RoBERTa. EN / UR / Roman UR.
→
03 · conditionalSarcasmFires only at 0.40–0.70 confidence.
→
04 · conditionalClustering3+ near-identical posts ⇒ campaign.
→
05 · conditionalLegal MappingAttaches statutory citation.
→
06 · alwaysHuman ReviewOfficer confirms, dismisses or escalates.

Dashed nodes fire conditionally on confidence and risk tier. The terminus does not.

03 — The Governing Constraint

The system cannot act on its own

This is not a policy commitment that could be relaxed later. It is enforced in the data layer.

  • Enforced at the databaseA constraint rejects any case record that attempts to bypass human review. There is no code path and no configuration setting that can disable it.
  • Advisory legal outputStatutory mapping is a drafting aid for a legally trained reviewer to confirm — never a determination. The interface states this on every case.
  • No fabricated citationsWhere a provision cannot be verified, the system records "not yet mapped" rather than proposing one.
  • Full audit trailEvery decision records the officer, timestamp, decision and written reasoning — available for departmental or judicial scrutiny.
Accountability boundary

requires_human_review
INTEGER NOT NULL
DEFAULT 1
CHECK (requires_human_review = 1)

Every case file carries this constraint. A record that tried to skip review would be rejected by the database itself.

Flagged content, matched to statute

Constitution of PakistanArticle 20Freedom of religion — recorded as the competing right to be weighed
Gilgit-Baltistan local lawNot yet mappedStated explicitly rather than guessed

Article 20 is carried deliberately. A system that only ever cites the offence, and never the right being weighed against it, is an enforcement tool rather than a legal one.

The published 95.7% accuracy figure was obtained on the model's original academic test set. It has not been validated on Gilgit-Baltistan content, and real-world accuracy on local dialects should be expected to be lower until retraining is complete. No accuracy guarantee is offered on that basis.

06 — Approach

One year. One approved figure.

A single twelve-month engagement covering all development, hardware and twelve months of subscriptions — with an early review point at Month 4, before the greater part of the money is committed.

Stage 1 · Months 1–3

Validation

Gilgit & Skardu. Delivers a measured accuracy report on the Department's own content. PKR 6.93M.

Stage 2 · Months 4–12

Build & rollout

Model retraining, security hardening, authentication, remaining districts, officer training. PKR 22.96M.

Year 2 onward

Not sought here

Recurring charges shown for transparency only, to be submitted separately when lower-cost options may exist.

The two stages are one approved figure of PKR 29,894,666, not two purchases. Stage 1 is the first three months of it.
